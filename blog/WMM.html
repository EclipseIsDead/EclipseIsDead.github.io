The World Master's Manual: An Ontology-Driven Worldbuilding Environment

Home Blog

The World Master's Manual (WMM for short) is an environment for people to build their world, define concepts within said world, record relationships between concepts, infer conclusions from said concepts and relationships, and check their world's consistency.

More on why it doesn't need to be Lean-like later...

Think of it like a steerable Dwarf Fortress, but with a worse UI. It's a tool with which you can design your world, and then work with an agent to push said world forward. I'd like to call it "Lean for Loremasters" but it's not a proof checker, nor does it need to be.

Why World Master Manual?

I run a Dungeons and Dragons campaign that's been going on for over a year and a half now. My general workflow is whenever I have an idea I write it down (this takes anywhere from 5 minutes to 3 hours) and then sit on it for at least a week. If I still like it, and there are no holes, and it seems fun then I keep it. But after doing this for so long, the sheer bloat of my Obsidian Repo has reached a point where I consistently run into:
